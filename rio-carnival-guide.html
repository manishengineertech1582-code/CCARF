Carnival 2027

What's Happening When

Official Carnival runs February 5–10, with the Champions' Parade on February 13. Your dates (Feb 6–11) land squarely inside peak week, including all three Special Group Sambadrome nights.

Carnival-Specific Notes

  • Avenida Atlântica (beachfront) closed to vehicles on major bloco days
  • Some of Rio's largest blocos happen on your doorstep — you live inside the party
  • Use interior streets for Uber pickup (Rua Barata Ribeiro, Rua Ministro Viveiros de Castro)
  • Metro: Cardeal Arcoverde and Siqueira Campos are 5–10 min walk from most hotels
  • Pickpocketing in bloco crowds is the primary risk — use a money belt, take a cheap phone
  • Returning from Sambadrome: metro to Siqueira Campos, then short walk
Verdict: Best choice for Carnival immersion and accommodation variety. Accept the crowd and noise as part of the experience. Choose a building on an interior street with a doorman. Prioritize apartments with verified security. The metro directly connects you to the Sambadrome.

Getting There

Sambadrome Access

On Special Group parade nights (Feb 7, 8, 9), the metro runs 24 hours. This is your best option — not Uber. Here's what you need to know.

From Copacabana

Siqueira Campos or Cardeal Arcoverde → transfer Central do Brasil

~30 min

From Ipanema

General Osório → transfer Central do Brasil

~35 min

From Botafogo

Botafogo → direct Line 2 or transfer at Central

~25 min

From Leblon

No metro — Uber to General Osório then metro, or Uber direct

45–80 min
🚇

Strategy: Take metro to the Sambadrome (Praça Onze for even sectors, Central do Brasil for odd sectors). After the parade ends at 5–6 am, walk 2–3 blocks clear of the main exit crowd before opening the Uber app — you'll find drivers and shorter wait times. Metro home is also valid; trains run continuously.

⚠

Do not: Try to hail a taxi or order Uber directly at the Sambadrome exit. You will join 40,000+ people competing for the same rides. Walk clear of the perimeter before requesting.

📱

Install 99 Pop alongside Uber — it's a local competitor that is often cheaper, and having both increases your chance of finding a driver quickly on surge nights. Expect 2–3× surge pricing from Uber on parade nights.

Best for Late-Night Returns
Copacabana or Botafogo

Both have metro access to/from the Sambadrome at 5 am. From Copacabana, walk from Siqueira Campos. From Botafogo, walk from Botafogo station. Both are safer at 5 am on metro nights than Uber from the Sambadrome entrance.

If I were booking this trip:

I would stay in Copacabana, on an interior street (not the beachfront avenue), in a building with a doorman, for the full stretch of February 6–11. Here's why:

The 2027 dates are all Sambadrome parade nights. The metro to the Sambadrome from Copacabana is direct, 24-hour, and reliable — it's the only transportation option that doesn't fail on a night when 40,000 people are trying to leave at the same time. Copacabana also has the highest concentration of quality 2-bedroom apartments available during Carnival, and you're within walking distance of some of Rio's biggest street parties without needing Uber at all.

I would not choose Leblon despite it being the safest neighborhood, because the lack of metro is a genuine liability during Sambadrome nights. I would consider Ipanema as a close second — slightly safer, slightly harder metro access, more expensive accommodation.

My first call: The 4.78★ Airbnb listing with 2 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms. Check it today, verify queen beds, verify exact location, and book if it's in Copacabana or Ipanema. After that: the Arpoador 2BR listing — that Ipanema/Copacabana border location is rare and gives you the best of both neighborhoods.
